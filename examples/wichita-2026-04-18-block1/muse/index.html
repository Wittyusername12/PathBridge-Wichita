The Muse — BUILD THE TRUST AND ADOPTION SYSTEM

You have $100K for marketing. Build messaging, trust, and adoption that makes a no-wrong-door pathway usable by people in crisis and credible to landlords and providers.

The brief asksOur answerWhere on this page
Youth-friendly outreach kit The youth kit goes out through CrossRoads, DCF IL and BRIDGES, led by peer navigators with lived experience. It holds a pocket ID checklist that turns the 31-day document chain into plain steps, a bus-pass voucher guide, and a KanCare keep-your-coverage card. The goal is to cut lost contact below 20%, against DCF IL's 38%. —
Landlord trust campaign Landlord Luis asked for one clear contact, less risk and support that lasts, so every NEXTenant landlord gets a named Relay contact and a fact sheet on rapid rehousing and Housing First supports. We run landlord briefings every 2 weeks and aim to sign 15 new landlords by day 90. We will share Wichita's retention rate of 80% to show tenants stay housed. —
Bilingual pathway messaging Every script, wallet card, flyer and text message is written in English and Spanish at a 6th-grade level. 211 and Open Door already support Spanish, so Relay builds on them with Spanish radio spots and transit posters. The target is 20% of contacts served in Spanish. Bilingual flyer
Anti-stigma comms The anti-stigma series tells real stories from youth, parents and veterans, with their consent. It points out that 69% of people served were newly homeless, so homelessness can happen to any neighbor. Messages focus on what people are working toward, like school, a job and a home, not on labels. —
Brand system Relay Wichita uses a baton-handoff mark to show that no person is dropped between services. The brand has a warm color set, plain bilingual wording and the handle @RelayWichita, with co-branding space for Second Light, Open Door, H.O.T., CrossRoads, BRIDGES, DCF IL, City ID and NEXTenant. The promise is simple: every door leads home. 04 Creative Assets

🤝 Partner Recruitment Pitch

Stop losing people between your door and the next one.

Wichita runs 8 intake doors, yet 195 people slept outside on the 2025 count night. Roughly every third caller to 211 ends up in a loop, because there is no way to hold a warm handoff across agencies after hours. The family intake queue runs 2-4 weeks, and only 17 of 87 emergency beds are reachable for people living outside.

Relay Wichita assigns each youth, family, veteran and landlord one named contact who carries the referral from your door to a confirmed placement. In a 90-day pilot, 2 relay staff work alongside 211, Open Door, CrossRoads, H.O.T. outreach and VASH partners. Together they close the documents, benefits and bed-matching gaps that cause people to drop out.

Partnership Benefits

  • Warm handoffs replace cold referrals: your staff hand off to a named person, not a phone tree
  • Fewer repeat visits from people who have already cycled through another partner that day
  • Document navigation from birth certificate to state ID, starting from no documents
  • Coverage checks at KanCare and DCF Independent Living transition points, where youth most often drop off
  • A planned next step for families before the 90-day family shelter limit runs out
  • Landlords get one named contact for tenant issues after move-in
  • Shared outcome reporting your board and funders can use

🤖 How This Campaign Was Built — AI Process

Who this serves: Relay Wichita serves youth aging out of foster care, families and veterans facing homelessness, and the landlords and providers who help them find a home.

Built by a Python harness that fetches the challenge data and makes two structured calls to Claude (Anthropic API); Python checks each answer against a schema, builds this page and computes the reach calculator.

1. Data Ingestion — 7 challenge API endpoint(s) fetched at run time; a summary goes to the model, trimmed to a fixed length: each endpoint's record count and first three records by one rule for every shape (a list under “data”, “alerts” or another record key, a bare list, an object as one record), with the envelope's own fields such as an alert feed's status.

2. Audience Research — The first call (strategy) drafts the personas, each with the figures it cites, plus the channel plan, the calendar and the KPIs.

3. Creative Generation — The second call writes the bilingual copy (English + Spanish), the social posts and the partner pitch as structured output.

4. Channel Strategy — The prompt asks the model to match each channel to the personas' barriers (for example, digital and community partners rather than print where people lack a car).

5. Ask Atlas AI — Claude answers questions about this campaign from a context file built from it, through a server-side proxy; no key is on the page.
