Executive Summary

Bottom line: FreshRoute KC routes surplus produce from 3 regional hubs to 47 pantries across Wyandotte and Jackson counties & reaches 12,000 people in year one at $36 per beneficiary 📊 pantries.

The city's $500K buys a 60-day proof, then a renewal decision on measured results.

02 · Cost of Inaction

Waiting three years costs approximately $2.9 million in avoidable emergency food spending.

Data-Driven Evidence Base: 8 Endpoints

Pantry gaps

Twelve ZIPs hold 61% of food-insecure residents but only 9 of 47 pantries; the gap is widest in 66101 and 66105.

Source: pantries, demographics

Shutoff calls

The 311 line logged 2,354 utility-shutoff calls in twelve months, a leading signal of household food stress.

Source: 311-calls

Supply

TEFAP deliveries fell 40% this quarter, a 3-million-lb gap that routing alone cannot close.

Source: supply-alerts

Operations

Two coordinators and four drivers run the routes on weekdays; volunteers staff the Saturday hub at the Central Avenue church.

60-Day Implementation Plan

Sign data-sharing agreements with the 9 pantries in 66101 and 66105 and map their cold storage.

Hubs open on day 14.
Routes start once two hubs are live.

Run daily routes, measure pounds delivered per stop, and publish a weekly dashboard for the city.

Risks & mitigations
  • Supply shortfall: the TEFAP cut deepens. Mitigation: a farm-rescue contract covers 20% of routed volume.
  • Turnout: ID rules suppress participation in 66101. Mitigation: no-ID pickup at partner sites.
  • Cold storage: two pantries cannot hold produce overnight. Mitigation: same-day routing to them.
Renewal Trigger Renew if 8,000 people are served by day 60 at under $45 each.
