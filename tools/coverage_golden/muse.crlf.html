02 · Channel Strategy
How We Reach Them
WhatsApp groups — weekly drop alerts in Spanish and English, sent by trusted parish volunteers.
Bus shelters — QR posters on the 101 and 107 lines, where riders wait an average of 14 minutes.
Church bulletins — a short notice each Sunday in the six parishes nearest the hubs.
